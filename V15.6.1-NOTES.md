# CloudPad Studio (Dev) v15.6.1

## Fixed: waiting extended the preceding hold
At a shared boundary (for example, RT/LS release at 2.6 s and B press at 2.6 s), editing B's wait incorrectly moved those earlier releases too. Moving subsequent actions now moves complete logical actions rather than all events after a timestamp. Earlier holds keep their original release times, including simultaneous or overlapping holds.

The Wait before action anchor now uses the latest end of all preceding actions, instead of the previous row's start or an incomplete endpoint. Changing a wait from 0 to 0.5 seconds moves the selected and subsequent actions while preserving their durations.

## Explicit neutral waits
- Selected actions now offer Insert neutral wait presets: +0.1, +0.5 and +1.0 seconds.
- An insertion at a shared boundary leaves the preceding releases in place and moves new presses after the wait.
- Holds crossing the insertion are explicitly released during the wait and resumed afterward at their original analog levels. Existing later timing shifts by the inserted duration. This deliberately creates a period with all buttons released and axes centered.
- Neutral gaps are visible in a separate striped Wait lane.
- Existing signed percentage controls, timeline editing and precise event mode remain available.

## Validation
Pure logic and browser tests reproduce the shared-boundary defect, repeated wait changes, parallel-action anchoring, neutral saved playback, held RT/LS pause and exact-depth restoration, analog transitions at the cut, total duration, atomic rejection of invalid insertions, timeline dragging/resizing, percentage controls, error focus, draft save/name semantics and bilingual UI. The existing right-click isolation regression also passes.

Development build; stable Release unchanged.
